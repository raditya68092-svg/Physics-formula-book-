Formulas • Short Differences • Quick Revision

1. Electric Charges and Fields ▼

Coulomb's Law: F = (1 / 4πε₀) × (q₁q₂ / r²)
Electric Field: E = F/q
Point Charge: E = (1 / 4πε₀) × (q/r²)
Electric Flux: Φ = EA cos θ
Gauss's Law: Φ = q/ε₀
Dipole Moment: p = q(2a)
Torque on Dipole: τ = pE sin θ

2. Electrostatic Potential and Capacitance ▼

Potential: V = W/q
Point Charge Potential: V = (1/4πε₀)(q/r)
Capacitance: C = Q/V
Parallel Plate Capacitor: C = ε₀A/d
Energy: U = ½CV² = ½QV = Q²/2C

3. Current Electricity ▼

Current: I = Q/t
Ohm's Law: V = IR
Resistance: R = ρL/A
Power: P = VI = I²R = V²/R
Drift Relation: I = neAvd

4. Moving Charges and Magnetism ▼

Magnetic Force: F = qvB sin θ
Current-Carrying Wire: F = BIL sin θ
Radius: r = mv/qB
Long Straight Wire: B = μ₀I/(2πr)
Torque on Loop: τ = NIAB sin θ

5. Magnetism and Matter ▼

Magnetic Dipole Torque: τ = MB sin θ
Potential Energy: U = −MB cos θ

6. Electromagnetic Induction ▼

Magnetic Flux: Φ = BA cos θ
Faraday's Law: ε = −dΦ/dt
Self Induction: ε = −L(di/dt)
Inductor Energy: U = ½LI²

7. Alternating Current ▼

AC Voltage: V = V₀ sin ωt
RMS Voltage: Vrms = V₀/√2
RMS Current: Irms = I₀/√2
Inductive Reactance: XL = ωL
Capacitive Reactance: XC = 1/(ωC)
Impedance: Z = √[R² + (XL−XC)²]

8. Electromagnetic Waves ▼

Speed: c = 1/√(μ₀ε₀)
Wave Relation: c = νλ

9. Ray Optics and Optical Instruments ▼

Mirror Formula: 1/f = 1/v + 1/u
Mirror Magnification: m = −v/u
Lens Formula: 1/f = 1/v − 1/u
Lens Magnification: m = v/u
Power: P = 1/f (f in metre)
Refractive Index: n = c/v

10. Wave Optics ▼

Fringe Width: β = λD/d
Bright Fringe: Path difference = nλ
Dark Fringe: Path difference = (2n+1)λ/2

11. Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter ▼

Photon Energy: E = hν = hc/λ
Photoelectric Equation: hν = φ + Kmax
de Broglie Wavelength: λ = h/p

12. Atoms ▼

Bohr Angular Momentum: mvr = nh/2π
Energy: En = −13.6/n² eV (hydrogen)

13. Nuclei ▼

Mass-Energy: E = mc²
Decay Law: N = N₀e−λt
Half-Life: T½ = 0.693/λ
Activity: A = λN

14. Semiconductor Electronics ▼

Diode Equation: I = I₀(eeV/kT − 1)
Revision: Diode, rectifier, transistor aur logic gates ke symbols aur truth tables bhi revise karein.

⚡ Important Short Differences ▼

TopicDifference
AC vs DCAC ki direction/magnitude time ke saath vary karti hai; ideal DC ki direction constant hoti hai.
Conductor vs InsulatorConductor mein free charge carriers zyada available hote hain; insulator mein bahut kam.
Intrinsic vs Extrinsic SemiconductorIntrinsic pure semiconductor hai; extrinsic mein controlled impurity doping hoti hai.
Convex vs Concave LensConvex lens parallel rays ko converge karta hai; concave lens diverge karta hai.
Interference vs DiffractionInterference multiple coherent wave contributions se intensity pattern banata hai; diffraction aperture/edge ke around wave spreading se pattern banata hai.
Mass Defect vs Binding EnergyMass defect mass difference hai; binding energy nucleus ko separate nucleons mein todne ke liye required energy hai.
